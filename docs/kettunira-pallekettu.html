Kettunira / Pallekettu (The Preparation)

The preparation stage brings the vow into practical form. Kettunira and Pallekettu are associated with the final readiness for the pilgrimage, especially the preparation of the Irumudi and the collection of items used for the temple journey. It becomes a visible expression of faith, gratitude, and spiritual expectation.

Bloomington Hindu Temple

  • Prepare the Irumudi with care, prayer, and guidance from local elders or temple contacts.
  • Confirm group travel timing, essentials, and household support.
  • Pack with intention and focus, keeping the path to the temple in mind.

St. Louis Temple

  • Review temple guidance and practical travel arrangements for the final pilgrimage.
  • Prepare the sacred offerings and clothing with care and gratitude.
  • Stay organized so the energy of the vratam remains calm and clear.

Saranam Yatra

  • Pack travel essentials and sacred items in a way that reflects humility and readiness.
  • Keep the route organized but centered on prayer and spiritual purpose.
  • Make the preparation itself a devotional act that supports the final journey.

Plainfield Temple

  • Align with the temple's practical guidance for the final preparation.
  • Organize the Irumudi and essentials carefully before departure.
  • Prepare the mind for a calm and respectful pilgrimage experience.

Community / Guruswamy Route

  • Receive guidance from a guru or elder on what to pack and how to prepare.
  • Make the Irumudi a personal symbol of surrender and trust.
  • Use this stage to settle both the body and the mind before departure.
